## 📋 Executive Summary

Today's milestone delivered the **High-Appeal Citizen Defense Suite**, comprehensive **Backend Robustness Hardening**, and a thorough **End-to-End Code Review** across the entire platform.

This release addresses the rapid rise of modern Indian cyber epidemics—notably "Digital Arrest" extortion, malicious APK sideload phishing, utility disconnection scams, and physical KYC document leakage—while expanding consumer delight with a gamified **Suraksha Citizen Score (0-1,000)**, **Apple & Google Wallet pass integration**, and a 1-tap **Emergency SOS Panic Pack**.

   - Pre-fills formal 1930 / `cybercrime.gov.in` complaint drafts citing IT Act Sections 66C/66D and BNS Sections 318/319.
2. **Sideload APK & Malicious SMS Phishing Radar:**
   - Detects dangerous Android `.apk` download links disguised as electricity bill updates (BESCOM, Mahadiscom, UPPCL) or traffic challans.
   - Flags suspicious personal sender numbers (+91 mobile numbers) posing as institutional telemarketer headers.
3. **Suraksha Citizen Score (0 to 1,000) & Privacy Health Ring:**
   - Dynamic 0-1,000 credit-score style privacy rating across 6 weighted security vectors.
   - Real-time tiering: Excellent (850-1,000), Strong (700-849), Moderate (500-699), and Needs Attention (<500).

## 🔒 Code Review & Robustness Findings

A rigorous code review was conducted covering the newly built services and existing codebase:

1. **Input Bounds & ReDoS Resistance:**
   - Added strict Pydantic field bounds on all requests (`max_length=10000` on message text, `min_length=1`, `max_length=50000` on QR payloads).
   - Regex patterns in `ScamRadarEngine` use linear character classes without nested quantifiers, eliminating polynomial backtracking.
2. **Thread Safety & Memory Protection:**
   - `EphemeralPassStore` operates with a reentrant `threading.Lock`.
   - Bounded cache capacity at 10,000 entries with automatic oldest-entry eviction prevents memory exhaustion under denial-of-service attempts.
3. **XXE Injection Protection:**
   - `PassVerifierService` inspects UIDAI QR XML using safe regular expressions instead of invoking an external entity XML parser, fully eliminating XML External Entity (XXE) vulnerabilities.
4. **Zero-Plaintext Invariant:**
   - Verified that no route or store persists unmasked 12-digit Aadhaar, biometric samples, or unencrypted documents in plaintext.
   - Masked formats (`XXXX-XXXX-1234`) and asymmetric public signatures are strictly adhered to.
5. **Statutory Compliance:**
   - UIDAI Circular on Masked Aadhaar (2022).
   - Section 29 & 43 of Aadhaar Act, 2016 (restriction on core biometric sharing and unauthorized secondary verification).
   - Section 63 of Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (forensic electronic certificate).
   - Sections 66C & 66D of Information Technology Act, 2000 (identity theft and personation).
