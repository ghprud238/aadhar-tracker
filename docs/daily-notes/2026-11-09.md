### Phase 5: Technical Deep-Dive — Aadhaar/PAN Tracking, Dark Web CTI & Legal/Compliance Matrix
**User Requests:**
- *"how are we really tracking AADHAR or PAN numbers?"*
- *"how are we monitorign when this is used in dark web?"*
- *"What are some rules and legal details that we need to keep track of?"*

1. **How Aadhaar & PAN Are Really Monitored:**
   - **No Live Transaction Feed Exists:** Documented that UIDAI and the Income Tax Department do not expose open transactional webhooks or event streams to commercial entities. UIDAI's Central Identities Data Repository (CIDR) operates solely in request-response auth mode for licensed AUAs/KUAs.
   - **Deterministic Correlation via Verified Identifiers:** Monitoring is driven by verified resident identifiers (email/phone). When intelligence providers (HIBP, Flare) detect an identity in database dumps (telecom, insurance, e-commerce breaches), the breach record explicitly enumerates compromised data classes (`["Aadhaar numbers", "PAN", "Passwords", "Financial Data"]`).
   - **Zero-Plaintext Storage:** Enforced by [`packages/security/crypto_vault.py`](file:///Users/dpcubed/projects/aadhar-tracker/packages/security/crypto_vault.py) using AES-256-GCM envelope vault encryption and HMAC-SHA256 blind indexing. No 12-digit Aadhaar is ever displayed or stored in plaintext; masked representations only (`XXXX-XXXX-1234` / `ABCDE****F`).
   - **Official Protective Action:** The platform does not claim to intercept bank transactions, but guides residents through UIDAI's official defense mechanisms: **Biometric Lock** (invalidates physical fingerprint/iris spoofing), **Virtual ID (VID)** generation, and **50-event authentication history audits**.
   - **Zero-Scraping Invariant:** Platform code strictly prohibits scraping government portals or intercepting resident OTPs ([`docs/threat-model.md`](file:///Users/dpcubed/projects/aadhar-tracker/docs/threat-model.md)).

2. **How Dark Web Monitoring Operates:**
   - **Licensed Threat Intelligence vs. Direct Crawling:** In accordance with [ADR-007](file:///Users/dpcubed/projects/aadhar-tracker/docs/decisions/ADR-007.md), direct Tor crawling or dark web bot deployment is prohibited to avoid criminal contamination and legal liability. Licensed CTI providers (**Flare Systems** and **HaveIBeenPwned**) are used.
   - **Indexed Data Sources:**
     - **Infostealer Malware Logs:** RedLine, Vidar, Racoon, Lumma logs stealing browser caches, saved credentials, autofill data, and locally stored identity scans (e-Aadhaar PDFs, PAN images).
     - **Underground Forum Dumps & Telegram Channels:** Bulk database dumps posted on dark web forums and Telegram cybercrime channels.
   - **Sanitization & Escalation:** Provider payloads are stripped of raw passwords and plain secrets before persistence ([`packages/redaction/masking.py`](file:///Users/dpcubed/projects/aadhar-tracker/packages/redaction/masking.py)). Detections containing Aadhaar/PAN automatically escalate to Critical severity (score 90+) in [`packages/domain/scoring.py`](file:///Users/dpcubed/projects/aadhar-tracker/packages/domain/scoring.py), triggering instant push alerts and wearable haptic feedback.

3. **Statutory Rules & Legal Compliance Matrix (India):**
   - **Aadhaar Act 2016 (and 2019 Amendments):**
     - *Section 29(1):* Core biometric information can never be collected, stored, or shared.
     - *Section 29(2) & 29(4):* 12-digit Aadhaar numbers must never be displayed publicly; only masked format (`XXXX-XXXX-1234`) is legally permitted.
     - *Section 38 & 42:* Severe penalties (up to 10 years imprisonment) for unauthorized access or tampering with CIDR.
   - **Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act):**
     - Platform operates as a *Data Fiduciary* requiring verifiable consent (Section 6), itemized notice in scheduled languages including Hindi (Section 5), strict data minimization and storage limitation (Section 8), and automated right-to-erasure and data portability (Sections 12 & 13, implemented via [`services/privacy_worker/`](file:///Users/dpcubed/projects/aadhar-tracker/services/privacy_worker/)). Penalties up to ₹250 Crores for non-compliance.
   - **Information Technology Act 2000 & SPDI Rules 2011:**
     - *Section 43A:* Mandates ISO 27001-grade security, AES-256 data encryption at rest, and TLS 1.3 in transit.
     - *Section 66F:* Prohibits unauthorized system intrusion, illegal scraping, or direct acquisition of stolen data.
   - **CERT-In Directives (April 2022):**
     - Mandatory reporting of cybersecurity incidents within 6 hours.
     - Secure retention of system, server, and audit logs within Indian jurisdiction for 180 days.
     - Mandatory system clock synchronization via NTP (NPL/NIC).
   - **Income Tax Act 1961 (PAN Regulations):**
     - Mandatory masking of sensitive PAN alphanumeric characters (`ABCDE****F`); prohibition of unauthorized PAN verification portal querying.
   - **PMLA & Ethical Intelligence Sourcing:**
     - Strict prohibition on purchasing stolen data from criminal actors or ransomware brokers; ingestion exclusively via contractually licensed defensive CTI feeds.

### Phase 10: Dark Web Aadhaar & PAN Monitoring Architecture & Legal Compliance Deep-Dive
**User Request:** *"how are we monitorong the dark web for aadhar or PAN cards"*

Conducted a comprehensive technical and legal architectural review of the dark web monitoring engine, detailing how Aadhaar and PAN credentials are tracked without violating privacy laws, without sending plaintext numbers to third parties, and without scraping government portals:

1. **The 3 Technical Pillars of Dark Web Monitoring:**
   - **Pillar A: Licensed Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) Ingestion ([`packages/provider_adapters/flare.py`](file:///Users/dpcubed/projects/aadhar-tracker/packages/provider_adapters/flare.py), [`packages/provider_adapters/hibp.py`](file:///Users/dpcubed/projects/aadhar-tracker/packages/provider_adapters/hibp.py)):**
     - Rather than directly crawling criminal forums or purchasing stolen dumps (prohibited by PMLA and IT Act), the platform ingests structured threat intelligence from contractually licensed defensive CTI feeds (Flare Threat Intelligence and HaveIBeenPwned Enterprise).
     - Crawlers index infostealer malware logs (Lumma, Vidar, RedLine) from infected consumer devices, identifying exfiltrated desktop KYC files (`e-Aadhaar.pdf`, `pan_card.jpg`) and browser autofill databases.
     - Crawlers index bulk third-party corporate breach dumps (telecom, NBFC, and fintech KYC tables) and catalog exposed data classes (`["Email", "Phone", "Aadhaar numbers", "PAN"]`).
   - **Pillar B: Mathematical Validation & Text DLP Engine ([`packages/domain/verhoeff.py`](file:///Users/dpcubed/projects/aadhar-tracker/packages/domain/verhoeff.py)):**
     - **Verhoeff Dihedral $D_5$ Algorithm**: Validates authentic 12-digit Aadhaar checksums and permutation tables, immediately suppressing bogus or corrupt dummy numbers.
     - **PAN Regex Structure Validator**: Validates 10-character alphanumeric PAN syntax (`[A-Z]{5}[0-9]{4}[A-Z]`) checking 4th character status code (`P` for individual) and 5th character last-name initial.
     - **Text DLP Scanner (`detect_identifiers_in_text`)**: Intercepts unmasked identifiers in pastebins, chat dumps, or simulated WhatsApp forwards and generates compliant masked strings (`XXXX-XXXX-1234` / `AB***12*F`).
   - **Pillar C: Zero-Plaintext Blind Indexing ([`packages/security/vault.py`](file:///Users/dpcubed/projects/aadhar-tracker/packages/security/vault.py)):**
     - Raw identifiers are stored in an AES-256-GCM Envelope Vault (DEK encrypted by KEK).
     - Generates a one-way deterministic Blind Index: $\text{Blind Index} = \text{HMAC-SHA256}(\text{Identifier}, \text{Tenant Secret Key})$.
     - Matching against ingested dark web observations uses blind indexes or verified correlation keys (e.g. verified email/phone hashes).
     - **Non-negotiable invariant**: Plaintext Aadhaar or PAN numbers are *never* transmitted over the internet to external APIs.

2. **Deterministic Risk Escalation & Multi-Surface Alert Delivery:**
   - Evaluated by [`packages/domain/scoring.py`](file:///Users/dpcubed/projects/aadhar-tracker/packages/domain/scoring.py):
