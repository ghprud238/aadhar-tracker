## ⏱️ Chronological Interaction & Milestones

### Phase 1: Strategic Blueprint for Usability & Expandability
**User Request:** *"what else can be added to the product to make it expanadable and usable?"*

1. **Strategic Product Horizons:**
   - **Horizon 1 (Multi-Document Coverage):** Expanded detection beyond Aadhaar and PAN to include Indian Driving License (Parivahan format), Voter ID (EPIC format), and Passport numbers.
   - **Horizon 2 (Purpose-Watermarked Passes):** Single-use masked identity passes for hotel check-ins, car rentals, and co-working spaces with burnt-in, indelible purpose watermarks (*"Valid only for Taj Lands End on 15-Nov-2026 • Not for Loan/KYC"*), preventing secondary fraud even if staff leak the document.
   - **Horizon 3 (National Cybercrime Emergency Toolkit):** 1-tap integration with the Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) **1930 Helpline** and automated legal complaint drafting under **Section 66C & 66D of the Information Technology Act, 2000** for `cybercrime.gov.in`.
   - **Horizon 4 (DigiLocker Integration):** Direct citizen consent connection to MeitY's DigiLocker for digital document import into the local Secure Enclave without paper scanning.
   - **Horizon 5 (Suraksha B2B Shield SDK):** Modular npm/React Native package allowing hospitality, fintech, and delivery apps to embed on-device UIDAI-compliant auto-masking into their own apps.
   - **Horizon 6 (Zero-Cloud P2P Family Mesh):** Offline Bluetooth/local Wi-Fi pairing allowing adult children to monitor elderly parents' biometric lock status without centralized cloud servers.

2. **Codebase Implementation ([`apps/mobile/src/services/`](file:///Users/dpcubed/projects/aadhar-tracker/apps/mobile/src/services/)):**
