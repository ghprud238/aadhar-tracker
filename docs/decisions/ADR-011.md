# ADR-011: Deterministic Explainable Prioritization

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Single opaque "security scores" (e.g. 74/100) are confusing to users and cannot be audited.

## Decision
Separate risk into distinct dimensions: Severity (consequence), Confidence (evidence strength), Freshness (observation age), and Coverage (which sources were checked). Provide deterministic, rule-based prioritization (0-100) with rule versioning.

## Consequences
Users understand why an incident was flagged and what actions to take.
