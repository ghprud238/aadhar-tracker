# ADR-014: Privacy Operations Independent of Billing Availability

## Status
Accepted

## Context
If a payment processor is down or a user has an overdue balance, they must never be prevented from exercising their privacy rights.

## Decision
Consent withdrawal, data export, and account deletion workflows are completely decoupled from billing status and remain operational regardless of payment gateway availability.

## Consequences
Guarantees compliance with DPDP and GDPR legal standards.
