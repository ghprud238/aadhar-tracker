# ADR-009: No Raw Leaked-Secret Retention

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Retaining plain passwords or session cookies turns the product into a high-value attack target and breaches data protection standards.

## Decision
Filter out and discard all plaintext passwords, session cookies, auth tokens, and raw biometric records during normalization. Only retain boolean indicators (`credential_material_present=True`) and exposure categories.

## Consequences
Protects user safety even in the event of an internal system compromise.
