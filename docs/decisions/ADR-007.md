# ADR-007: Licensed Intelligence Before Custom Collection

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Running raw web or Tor crawlers into criminal underground forums exposes the platform to severe legal, ethical, and operational liability.

## Decision
Use reputable, contractually approved licensed intelligence providers (HIBP for breach associations, Flare for dark-web intelligence) rather than in-house crawlers.

## Consequences
Reduces operational danger; necessitates provider contract compliance, rate-limiting, and capability registry management.
